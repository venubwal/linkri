## Services Included

Upon enrollment, LinkRi may provide multiple following services:

* Review and evaluation of your professional profile based on information available from LinkedIn, Naukri, and the resume provided by you.
* Resume assessment, ATS (Applicant Tracking System) compatibility review, profile scoring, and improvement recommendations.
* Resume modernization, restructuring, formatting enhancements, and content optimization to improve professional presentation and recruiter visibility.
* Identification and sharing of suitable job opportunities based on your skills, experience, and career preferences.
* Leveraging LinkRi's professional network to increase visibility of your profile among recruiters, hiring managers, and industry professionals.
* Sharing of your resume with relevant organizations, recruitment agencies, consulting firms, and hiring teams where appropriate.
* AI-assisted identification of potential employers, companies, and consulting organizations that may have suitable openings matching your profile.
* AI-assisted job application support across selected platforms, including LinkedIn, Naukri, company career portals, and other publicly available job sites, subject to platform policies and technical feasibility.
* Career guidance and recommendations to improve your chances of receiving interview calls and job opportunities more then before.
